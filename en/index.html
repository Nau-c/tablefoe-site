What TableFoe is

It's not just for Warhammer: TableFoe works for any tabletop wargame or board game, competitive or casual. Many players have an army or deck ready and want to play, but don't always know who else plays nearby. TableFoe connects those players through open games: post yours, anyone nearby can show interest, and you meet up to play.

FAQ

Is it only for Warhammer?

No. Any tabletop wargame or board game works, competitive or casual — you pick the system when you post or search a game.

Do I need to install anything?

No. TableFoe runs straight in your browser (it's a PWA) — tap "Open the web app" above. Your browser also lets you add it to your home screen like a native app.

How much does it cost?

The web app is free. The Android version is in closed testing, ahead of its Google Play launch.

Do I need to share my exact location?

No. It's only used to filter games by distance; the contact info you share (email, Discord, phone...) is your choice when posting or showing interest, not before.

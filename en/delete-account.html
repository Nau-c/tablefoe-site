Delete your TableFoe account and data

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

TableFoe is the app made by TableFoe (contact: soporte@tablefoe.app) for finding an opponent and playing Warhammer games. This page explains how to request the deletion of your account and personal data, what gets removed, and what we keep.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — From the app itself (instant)

Open TableFoe → sign in → tap the profile icon → scroll down to "Danger zone" → "Delete account" → confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted immediately, no waiting period.

Option 2 — Without the app installed

Email soporte@tablefoe.app from the address you registered with, stating "Delete my TableFoe account". We verify the request came from that account and delete it within 30 days at the latest.

What data gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Account (email, encrypted password)Yes, immediately
Profile (display name, game system, experience level, play style)Yes, immediately
Optional contact (Discord/WhatsApp you added)Yes, immediately
Open matches you've publishedYes, immediately
Ratings you've given or received from other playersYes, immediately
Push notification token (FCM)Yes, immediately

What we keep, and for how long

  • Database backups: after deletion, your information may persist residually in our provider's (Supabase) encrypted backups until those backups naturally rotate out, for a maximum of 30 days — we don't restore or query them except for disaster recovery.
  • Analytics and advertising data (Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob): these are aggregated events tied to a device/advertising identifier, not to your account or name, so deleting the account doesn't automatically delete them; Google retains them per its own policy (by default, up to 14 months in Firebase Analytics). You can also reset or delete your device's advertising identifier from Android settings at any time.

TableFoe Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

TableFoe ("the app", "we") has zero tolerance for any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE). This page describes our standards, how to report a concern, and how we handle it.

1. Zero tolerance

We do not allow content, behavior, or communication that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors, including any form of grooming, soliciting sexual content from a minor, or child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This applies to anything a user can post or communicate through TableFoe: profile name, match descriptions, and the contact info shared between players.

2. Who can use TableFoe

TableFoe is not directed at children under 16 (see our privacy policy). Creating an account requires accepting that the user meets that age requirement.

3. How to report a concern

If you see or suspect a case of child sexual abuse or exploitation related to TableFoe — a profile, a contact message, or any other interaction — email us immediately at soporte@tablefoe.app with as much detail as possible (username, match, screenshots if you have them). We review these reports with the highest priority.

4. What we do with a report

  • We review the reported account and content.
  • We immediately remove any account or content that violates this policy.
  • Where required by law, we report to the relevant authorities (including, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children — NCMEC — and law enforcement in the relevant jurisdiction) and cooperate with any related investigation.

5. Designated contact

Contact person for CSAE compliance and prevention: soporte@tablefoe.app.
